# Surface: Club Vault home (single page)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: people planning a night out on their phones, plus private-event hosts. Actions: call, reserve a table through that night's host, buy promoter tickets, send a private-event inquiry. Proof: the real weekly lineup, the Google listing's hours, "since 2010", and named event staff. Constraints: never invent prices, packages, menus, policies or reviews. Unknowns are shown as labeled placeholders.

Grounded list (ordered by resonance):
1. promoter flyer wall
2. bank-vault door hardware
3. wristband and ink entry stamp
4. bottle-service sparkler parade
5. marquee letterboard
6. Hollywood Beach deco neon
7. the black card / gold-foil stamped invitation (ASSIGNED)

Challenger verdicts (all declined):
- HyperCard stack: declined. Kept: every card is addressable (a deep link per night).
- Seven-segment clock: declined. Kept: the unlit state is designed too. Closed Monday and every unknown fact are shown as blind (unfoiled) debossed text, drawn as carefully as the lit parts.
- PC-98 field: declined. Kept: fixed regions never move (a persistent action bar).
- Saul Bass titles: declined. Kept: one idea per card, set in hard asymmetry.
- Provenance ribbon: declined. Kept: known and unconfirmed facts are visibly different states.
- Alien understory: declined. Kept: light answers attention. The foil sheen follows the pointer or scroll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the invitation itself: a black card with foil-stamped type. It refuses the category default of a neon-and-smoke hero photo over a centered CTA stack.

OWN-WORLD:
- Near-black stock (#0B0A08) with laminated card panels (#15130F).
- Gold foil as a material, not a color: a metallic sheen on stamped type and hairline rules. Blind deboss (tone-on-tone) marks unconfirmed or empty facts.
- Type: extended heavy caps (Archivo Expanded) as the foil stamp; Hanken Grotesk for reading.
- Controls are foil-edged plates. Radii are small, like a card's die-cut corner.

STORY:
- The visitor learns which night it is tonight and whether the doors are open.
- They believe this is a real, running room: five promoter nights, since 2010.
- They reserve through that night's host, call, or send a buyout inquiry.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A black card fills the screen.
- The CLUB VAULT wordmark is foil-stamped, set huge and extended, bleeding toward the edges on mobile.
- A live "Tonight" plate sits beneath it: the day, the night's name, doors-to-close hours, or "Closed tonight" in blind deboss.
- Primary action: Reserve a Table (gold plate). Secondary: Call. On mobile the sticky bar holds both.

FORM: #7 of the ordered list, seed key 1686b1a9. Signature interaction: the foil sheen tracks the pointer (on touch, it tracks scroll position) across stamped type, and Tonight's card lights up in the week strip. Motion is slow, one sheen sweep; reduced motion leaves it static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
