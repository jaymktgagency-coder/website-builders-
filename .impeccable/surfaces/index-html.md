## Scope

`index.html` — systim's single marketing surface. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: operations leaders at 50–500 person companies, arriving skeptical after
an internal automation attempt stalled; secondary, technical founders. Job: decide
whether to book a conversation. Action: book a conversation. Proof: none real yet —
honest placeholder slots enumerated in CONTENT.md. Constraint: static HTML/CSS, no
build step, readable with JavaScript off.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A running system, shown running — and then handed over. The page is a
process-control mimic panel: the visitor watches work move through a line that does
not stop, and the ritual it ends on is the shift handover, because client ownership
is the category's real objection. It refuses the two arrangements this category
always ships: near-black with a violet-to-cyan gradient headline and an abstract
orb, and its predictable opposite, a white page with a large serif and nothing
behind it.

OWN-WORLD: Warm instrument-panel white (#F4F2EE) as ground, warm graphite ink
(#16150F), hairline mimic rules (#C9C5BC) carrying the diagram, and one steady-state
signal green (#1B7F53) permitted only where a system reports live. Archivo carries
voice, its width axis narrowing for instrument labels; Chivo Mono carries readouts,
numerals, and system names — measurement only, never decoration. Elevation is
declared once, as a hairline, never a shadow. Depth is three planes: ground, rule
plane, content plane. Recognizable with all content removed by the hairline mimic
line and the green state dot alone.

STORY: The visitor understands that systim works inside the systems they already
run; believes it because the page names real ones and shows the process shape rather
than adjectives; and books a conversation. The turn is the handover section: the
thing they are buying is their own team's independence.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Lowercase wordmark top-left, one nav row right, hairline beneath at
full bleed. Below it a single sentence at display scale (clamp to 6rem ceiling,
-0.03em) held to the left five columns of a twelve-column bleed, the remaining seven
left empty — the confidence is the emptiness. The primary action sits inline at the
end of that sentence as a ruled link, not a floating pill. Beneath, spanning full
bleed, the mimic line: five labelled stages joined by hairlines, each carrying a
reference numeral tied to its explanation further down, with a single low-amplitude
pulse travelling left to right, continuously, proving throughput without hover.

FORM: The Control Room, candidate 3 of 7 on the grounded list, assigned by the roll
and locked by the user. Seed key 57393fa3. Raised by the hands it beat — numbered
reference marks on every stage (Patent Sheet, competitive); the handover artifact
shown before the call is asked for (Darkroom, declined); continuous motion rather
than hover-only (Plankton, declined); one idea per section (Fletcher, declined);
structural depth over a flat sheet (Multiplane, declined); show how the work is made,
not only what it produces (Riso, declined).

Signature interaction: the pulse on the mimic line is the page's one authored motion
moment. It runs on its own, halts entirely under reduced-motion with the line
rendered in its final state, and is the only thing on the page that moves by itself.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish
review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
