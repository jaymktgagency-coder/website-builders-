# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS, deployed to Vercel on a free `*.vercel.app` URL. Pinned by the owner's brief.

## Users

- **Night-out planners on their phones.** Mostly mobile, often deciding the same day or the same week, often in a group chat. They want to know what's on tonight, how to get a table, and how to get in touch.
- **Private-event hosts.** People planning a birthday, quinceañera, wedding, bar/bat mitzvah, corporate, holiday or day party who want to rent the venue.

## Product Purpose

Club Vault is a nightclub and private-event rental venue in Hollywood, FL. The website turns a visitor into a call, a table reservation, a promoter ticket purchase or a private-event inquiry.

## Positioning

Club Vault has been serving South Florida since 2010. On different nights the room is run by different promoter brands (Touch the Mic Tuesdays, Rum Veranda, SOS Thursdays, Tropical Fridayz, Catch a Vibe), and on other days it rents out as a private-event venue. It is one room with a different night every day of the week.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Table reservations go through phone or SMS to each night's host. Private-event inquiries are sent through FormSubmit to admin@megavaultinc.com.
- **Undecided or unknown. Show clearly labeled placeholders; never invent these:**
  - bottle service and table packages, and prices
  - drink and food menu, and prices
  - dress code
  - age policy
  - parking details
  - a Sunday event
  - music genres per night
  - Roger's phone number
- All photos are placeholder slots for the owner to swap.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Club Vault. Brief: premium, dark, moody; black with gold accents; bold type; subtle motion; high-end, not a template; mobile-first.
- Nothing from thevaulthollywood.com (a different venue) or any unrelated project may be reused.

## Product Principles

1. Tonight first: what's on and how to get in, within one thumb scroll.
2. Every claim traces to the listing or the owner. Unknowns are labeled, never filled in.
3. One tap to act: call, text or ticket from anywhere on the page.
4. The promoters' nights are the product. Give each night its own identity inside one house style.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on a dark ground. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Tap targets of at least 44px.
