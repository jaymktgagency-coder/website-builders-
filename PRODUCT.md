## Product Purpose

systim is a consultancy that integrates AI into the systems a company already
runs, and automates the work that moves between them. Success is a client
process that runs without a human in the loop, handed over with documentation
the client's own team can maintain.

## Positioning

Integration-first, not model-first. The work starts from the client's existing
stack and data, and ends with a handover rather than a dependency. The
differentiator claimed on the site is depth of integration into incumbent
systems plus an explicit exit: the client's team owns and operates what was
built.

## Operating Context

Buyers evaluate on desktop during a workday, often mid-comparison against other
vendors and internal build options. They will skim first and read second. Many
will forward a link to a technical colleague for a second opinion, so the site
must survive a skeptical engineering read as well as an executive skim.

## Product Principles

1. Earn the technical read. An engineer forwarded this link must find specifics,
   not adjectives.
2. Concrete over abstract. Name real systems, real process shapes, real
   sequence — never "leverage AI to transform your business."
3. The exit is the product. Handover and client ownership are stated plainly,
   because dependency is the category's main objection.
4. Restraint signals seniority. Confidence is shown by what is left out.
5. No borrowed credibility. Nothing on the page implies proof that does not
   exist yet.
