Most automation stalls at the integration.

The pilot works. The demo is convincing. Then it meets production: the record that four systems disagree about, the file that arrives by SFTP at 02:00, the step a person has to approve because a regulator says so. And it stops.

The model was never the hard part. The hard part is everything the model has to talk to, and the fact that all of it is already carrying your business. That is the work we do, and it is where we start.

How an engagement runs.

  1. 1

    Map

    Two weeks inside the operation. We follow one process end to end, timestamp every handoff, and come back with two things: the map, and the number. What it costs you to run this today, and which part of it is worth automating first.

    Fixed fee. You keep the map whether or not you continue.

  2. 2

    Build

    We build against your data, not a demo set. Model choice is an engineering decision, not a brand one. Most steps in a real process need retrieval, a schema and a rule; the two that genuinely need a frontier model get one.

  3. 3

    Integrate

    The output lands where the work already happens. No second dashboard to remember, no parallel process to reconcile at month end. If your team has to change tools to get the benefit, we have not finished.

  4. 4

    Prove

    It runs beside your existing process until the two agree. Every disagreement is logged and reviewed with your team, so you are reading a real error rate on your own data before anything is switched over.

    You decide when it takes over. Not us.

  5. 5

    Hand over

    Your team gets the runbook, the test suite, the access map and a week of pairing. Then we leave. If you bring us back, it is because you chose to, not because we are the only ones who understand it.
